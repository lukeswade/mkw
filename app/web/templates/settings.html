Search

When a source makes the cut, its most relevant outbound links become candidates too — the references a good page chooses are often better than anything a search engine returns.

Sites full of authoritative primary documents that search engines barely index. When one fits the topic, the planner dedicates a site:-scoped query to it.

Many CDNs reject non-browser clients on the TLS handshake alone. When a page answers 403/429/challenge, it is retried once presenting Chrome's exact fingerprint — no browser involved.

Last resort for pages that refuse even a real fingerprint: a FlareSolverr headless browser solves the challenge and returns the page. Start one with docker compose --profile browser up -d.
